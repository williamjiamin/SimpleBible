Simple Bible Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

Simple Bible is developed by LIPAI WANG for iOS and Android. It does not require an account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your reading history, notes, highlights, prayers, or AI Guide conversations.

On-device Bible and study data

Bible text, notes, highlights, reading plans, prayer-journal entries, history, settings, and AI Guide chat history are stored locally. The Android AI fallback is also processed locally. This content may be included in backups controlled by you and your platform provider.

Advertising

The free tier displays advertisements from Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may collect or share IP address for approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy.

On Android, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests consent where required. When Google requires a persistent privacy-options entry point, open Settings and select Privacy choices to review or change the available advertising choices.

Speech recognition

Voice search uses the speech-recognition service provided or configured by the operating system. Depending on your device and provider, spoken audio may be sent to that provider under its privacy terms. The developer does not receive the audio.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google through Google Play on Android. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Deleting data

Use available in-app deletion controls or clear App storage/delete the App to remove local data, subject to backups managed by you and your platform provider.
